How escrow works

  • You add funds to your wallet (demo deposit simulates a bank transfer).
  • When you book, you move the job amount into escrow — money leaves your wallet but stays on the platform.
  • Funds are not sent to the worker until they mark the job complete.
  • If the booking is cancelled, escrow is refunded to your wallet.
  • Platform fee: {{ platform_fee_percent }}% on release (deducted from the job amount).

Production: connect Stripe or your bank via a licensed payment provider. This build uses an internal ledger for demonstration.
