How do I find a professional?

Create a free client account, go to Find Professionals, filter by skill and location, then book and pay into escrow.

How does escrow work?

Your payment is held securely until the job is completed. Then funds are released to the worker (minus the platform fee). Cancellations refund escrow to your wallet.

How do I become a worker?

Sign up with Worker Signup, add your skills and service area, then accept booking requests from clients.

Is my payment information safe?

We use wallet and escrow ledger on ProTech. For live card payments, we will integrate a licensed processor (e.g. Stripe).

How do reviews work?

After a completed job, clients can leave a 1–5 star review. Worker ratings appear on their profile.
